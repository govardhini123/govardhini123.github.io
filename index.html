About

I know where the data comes from, because I've built the devices that produce it.

I'm an IoT Developer at DigiToad Technologies, Bengaluru, where I've spent 3+ years connecting STM32, ESP32 and Raspberry Pi devices to the cloud. Every one of those devices produces telemetry, and most of my work is getting that data off the device, into AWS and into a shape people can use.

On AWS that means IoT Core and IoT Rules for ingestion, Lambda for processing, S3 and DynamoDB for storage, Glue and Athena for transformation and queries, and Redshift for analytics. I use Python and SQL every day to clean, aggregate and structure sensor data for reporting.

I hold the AWS Certified Data Engineer – Associate certification and I'm moving into a dedicated data engineering role. To go deeper, I build projects like a batch ETL pipeline with star schema modeling in PostgreSQL.

I'm also an IBM Qiskit Advocate, and a 2023 graduate of Don Bosco Institute of Technology, Bengaluru.
